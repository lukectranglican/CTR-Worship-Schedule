Connect to Google Sheets

A one-time setup. Once connected, edits sync to a sheet you control, and the whole team sees the same data.
  1. Open sheets.google.com and create a blank spreadsheet. Name it whatever you like (e.g., "CTR Worship Schedule").
  2. In your sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script. A new tab will open with a code editor.
  3. Delete the placeholder function myFunction() {} and paste the code below. Then click the save icon (or Ctrl/Cmd-S).
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear icon ⚙ next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
  5. Configure: Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone. Then click Deploy.
  6. The first time you'll see a permissions prompt. Click Authorize access, choose your Google account, click Advanced → Go to (project name), then Allow. (Google warns because it's your own script — this is normal.)
